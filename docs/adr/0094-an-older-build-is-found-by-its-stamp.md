# ADR-0094 — An older build is found by its stamp; a left `theflow.md` is reported, not stopped on

**Status:** accepted. Implements #35. Corrects the factual premise of
[ADR-0067](0067-the-theflow-migration-path-is-gone.md), not its decision.

`grill-the-graph` §2 and `salvage` recognise an older generated build by a build
stamp that carries a revision, with a case-sensitive
`git grep -l -E '(Build stamp|Built from thegraph|빌드 스탬프)[^<]{0,40}[0-9a-f]{7}'`.
The two paths `scripts/thegraph/` and `.claude/agents/thegraph-*` still count.
Either sends the run to `salvage`, and a stamped `docs/agents/thegraph.md` is that
old build, not an earlier setup. A `docs/agents/theflow.md` does not stop the run:
`grill-the-graph` says it is there, and `salvage` clears it when the maintainer
asks, moving its tables first and finding what still cites it.

## The calls, and whose they were

The maintainer's, on 2026-10-28.

- At the confirm stop they rejected #35's proposal to cite `theflow.md`'s Step 1
  as evidence: *"theflow도 없어야하는건데"*. The compile path stays gone.
- The completeness read (`lens`, fresh agent) then found that two repositories
  still use the file: `justerm` records the decision to keep it as a cited corpus
  (its `CLAUDE.md`, 22 files citing it), and `frameee`'s CI, gate scripts and a
  test read its Step 7 gate matrix. Shown that, the maintainer chose *report, do
  not stop*, with clearing left to them per repository. The first call was made
  without that fact, so this one replaces it.

## What it was decided on

- **The path test missed a real build.** Stamps took at least five forms across
  versions and languages, under names the old skill never fixed:
  `Generated by /grill-the-graph. Build stamp: <rev>`, `# Build stamp: thegraph @
  <rev>`, `**Build stamp:** \`thegraph@<rev>\``, `Built from thegraph@<rev>.`, and
  `// 빌드 스탬프: thegraph/SKILL.md sha256 <rev>`. On 2026-10-28 two repositories
  on disk held one. `portfolio` has 7 stamped files under
  `.claude/agents/graph-*.md` and `scripts/graph-*.sh` plus a stamped
  `docs/agents/thegraph.md`. The path test finds none of it, and the thegraph.md
  made the run read as an *update*. `mobile_init_project` has 10 stamped files,
  in Korean; its paths were caught already. `x509_cert_store` held a third until
  its migration commit `511a88c`.
- **The revision and the case are required.** Without the revision the pattern
  matched this repository's own skill text, which describes the stamp. Without
  case-sensitivity it would match `grill-map`'s lowercase map stamp in about 17
  repositories. As written, a sweep over every repository on this machine finds
  `portfolio` (8) and `mobile_init_project` (10) and nothing else, and 11 files in
  `x509_cert_store` at its pre-migration commit `56b5880`.
- **ADR-0067's premise was false.** It removed the bindings path because *"no
  consuming repository was found"* holding `docs/agents/theflow.md`. On
  2026-10-23 #35 named seven that did. On 2026-10-28, four repositories on disk
  hold one: `flutter_tweakcn_generator` (one of the seven), `justerm`,
  `mobile_init_project` and `frameee`. Three of the seven had deleted theirs; the
  other three are not on this machine. The removal stands; next time a path is
  called dead, the consumers are checked first.

## Not done here

Clearing `portfolio`, `mobile_init_project`, or any `theflow.md`: each is
`salvage` in its own repository, on the maintainer's word.
